# FocusFlow AI
### Reclaiming Attention in the Age of Doom Scrolling

FocusFlow AI is an AI-powered digital wellness platform designed to help users regain focus, reduce attention fatigue, and build healthier digital habits. Instead of simply tracking screen time or blocking applications, FocusFlow AI understands user behavior and provides personalized recommendations to improve concentration and productivity.

---

## Problem Statement

Modern digital platforms are engineered to maximize engagement through endless scrolling, notifications, and constant content consumption. This results in fragmented attention, reduced productivity, digital fatigue, and difficulty performing deep work.

Current digital wellness applications mainly monitor screen time or restrict usage, but they rarely identify *why* users lose focus or provide personalized interventions.

FocusFlow AI addresses this gap by combining behavioral analytics with artificial intelligence to help users develop sustainable attention habits.

## How It Works

1. User starts a focus session.
2. The system monitors digital behavior and activity.
3. AI identifies distraction patterns.
4. Personalized recommendations are generated.
5. Users receive actionable insights instead of generic reminders.
6. Progress is visualized through dashboards and reports.
